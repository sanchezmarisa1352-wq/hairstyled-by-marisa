Appointment policies

Your appointment with Marisa Sanchez takes place at Hottie Hair Salon, West Charleston, 7871 W. Charleston Blvd, Suite 160, Las Vegas, NV 89117.

Booking and appointment changes

  • Requests sent through this site are pending until Marisa confirms. Hottie Hair confirms appointments by text or email.
  • Give Hottie Hair at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations with less notice may be charged 50% of the service price. A missed appointment is charged the full service price. The salon reviews emergencies individually.
  • If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the salon may shorten your service if it can still fit you in, or ask you to reschedule.

To make a change, call or text Hottie Hair at (702) 979-4468.

Consultations, pricing and payment

Hottie Hair requires an in-person consultation for major color and extension services. Online prices are estimates. Your quote may go up or down depending on your hair and the work or products needed. Marisa will review it with you before service starts.

Payment is due at the appointment. Hottie Hair lists cash, credit and debit cards for services. Gratuity is optional.

Aftercare and service concerns

Share relevant hair history and allergies with your stylist. Follow the aftercare instructions you receive, as they can affect coverage under the salon’s service guarantee.

If you’re unhappy with your service, contact Hottie Hair within seven days to discuss a resolution. Hottie Hair handles corrections at its salon.
